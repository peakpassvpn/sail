## sail 0.18.1 (pre-release)

A patch release on top of 0.18.0: five fixes, nothing else. There are no
breaking changes; configuration, the C ABI and the Swift and Kotlin APIs
are as in 0.18.0.

Documentation: https://peakpassvpn.github.io/sail/

### Fixes

- **AnyTLS and mux: a session cut short is not used again.** A stream
  dropped before the server's FIN left its session looking healthy, and
  the next connection on it waited and failed. Such a session is now
  retired, and a stream stuck on a reused session is opened once more on
  a fresh one.
- **A UDP reply from `::1` stays `::1`.** It was given back to the client
  as `0.0.0.1`.
- **TUN: an app learns at once when sail closes its connection.** When
  sail closed a TUN connection itself (a change of network, the Clash
  API, a reload's recheck, the instance stopping), the app's side could
  hear nothing and wait for its own timeout. It now gets a reset within a
  second.
- **TUN: many connections opened at once are not reset under memory
  pressure.** Pressure reclaimed every connection still in its handshake,
  and with thousands opening at once (a phone's apps after a change of
  network) many were refused. Only handshakes that went stale are
  reclaimed now, and a reply sets aside the size of a header, not a full
  packet.
- **TLS: a half-close sends only close_notify.** Shutting down the
  sending side of a TLS stream also shut down the TCP connection, so
  sail's side was left in TIME_WAIT. It now sends close_notify alone,
  and the peer closes the TCP first.
