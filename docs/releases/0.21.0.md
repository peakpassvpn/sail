## sail 0.21.0 (pre-release)

A minor release on top of 0.20.0.

Documentation: https://peakpassvpn.github.io/sail/

### Upgrading from 0.20

- **`netstack.max_queues` is gone** (`--set netstack.max_queues=…` is an
  error): the TUN inbound's `multi_queue` asks for more than one queue.

### Default changes

- **The TUN opens one queue unless its `multi_queue` (sing-box 1.15's) is
  set,** on every profile: the desktop profile opened 4 and the server
  profile 8, and one queue measured faster in every case tried (Trojan
  over TUN, 2 and 4 cores: 2 queues 3–9% slower, 4 queues 4–9%). The
  runtime option `netstack.max_queues` is gone; `multi_queue: true` opens
  a queue to each CPU, eight at most (Linux).
- **The router profile's relay buffers grow to 64 KiB,** not 16
  (`relay.buffer_max_size`): a Trojan-over-TUN download went from 0.81 to
  1.03 of sing-box's on one core, at a fifth less CPU a GiB. An idle
  session holds none; a session whose writer stalls holds 64 KiB each way,
  64 MiB if all 512 sessions the profile allows stall at once.

### New

- **HTTP proxy over HTTP/2** (sing-box 1.15's): an `http` outbound with
  `version: 2` speaks HTTP/2 CONNECT over TLS, its tunnels requests on up
  to 8 pooled connections, and falls back to HTTP/1.1 where the server
  picks no `h2` (`disable_version_fallback` makes that an error); unset
  stays HTTP/1.1 as sing-box 1.14, and REALITY gets HTTP/1.1, warned of;
  an `http` inbound serves HTTP/2 beside HTTP/1.1 (`version`), over TLS
  or with prior knowledge, as does `mixed`. Their HTTP/2 fields
  (`idle_timeout`, `keep_alive_period`, `stream_receive_window`,
  `connection_receive_window`, `max_concurrent_streams`) are read.
- An inbound's TLS may serve a self-signed certificate made at start
  (`tls.self_signed`, a sail extension), as Mihomo's Hysteria2 and TUIC
  listeners do without one. Clients cannot verify it: they need `insecure`
  or a pin (`certificate_sha256`, `certificate_public_key_sha256`), and
  the pin changes with each new certificate, made at every start and on a
  reload that changes the inbound beyond its users and client-certificate
  checks, as a changed `tls.server_name` does.
- **Clash/Mihomo profiles:** `tunnel` and `tun` listeners are inbounds of
  their own, a tunnel's a direct inbound to its `target`, a tun's a TUN
  with Mihomo's listener defaults (no routes or DNS hijack unless set);
  `IN-TYPE` names them and the trojan, vless, vmess, hysteria2, tuic and
  anytls listeners, and `IN-TYPE,TUN` the tun listeners as well as `tun`.
- **Clash/Mihomo profiles:** a hysteria2 or tuic listener without
  `certificate` and `private-key` serves a self-signed one
  (`tls.self_signed`), as Mihomo's makes one up, where it was an error.

### Fixed

- Clash/Mihomo profiles are read as YAML 1.2, as Mihomo reads them: a
  plain `N`, `on` or `yes` is a string, so a group or listener named `N`
  is no longer named `false`; a boolean field still takes them.
- The CLI watches SIGTERM, SIGINT and SIGHUP before its listeners take
  connections: one sent as soon as a port answered, as a service manager
  may, ended the process unhandled, without draining or reloading.
