fn prepare_listener( &self, _socket: SockRef<'_>, _network: Network, ) -> Result<()>

Sets the options a socket it listens on on network needs, once the socket is bound. An inbound taking traffic the system diverts to it (tproxy) must mark its sockets transparent; most need nothing.

Source

fn accepted(&self, _socket: SockRef<'_>, _sess: &mut Session) -> Result<()>

Fills in sess from the socket of a connection its listener accepted, before its stream handler sees the connection. A connection diverted by the system (redirect) carries its destination on its socket rather than in its bytes.
